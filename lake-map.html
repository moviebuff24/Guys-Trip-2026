3 Nights on the Water

Our float plan for Aug 7–10, built around the lower lake near State Dock — so we're not burning days cruising 100 miles. The plan front-loads a secluded-cove night and a marina/party-cove sample so we figure out early which vibe we actually want. Tap any pin for details.

Numbered pins are our stops; gray pins are marinas & scenic spots along the way (not overnight stops). Route lines are straight-line sketches, not exact boat paths, and cove anchors are suggested zones — we'll pick the actual spot on the water (and State Dock staff will know which coves are quiet vs. packed that weekend).

Day by Day
Thu
Aug 7

Board & disappear Cove test · night 1

Check in at State Dock ~3pm, load ice, food and drinks at the Ship Store (biggest on the lake), and get off the dock before the 6pm cutoff. Short shakedown cruise to a quiet cove near the dam — nose into the bank, tie off to the trees, grill dinner.

Night one is pure seclusion. That's half our cove-vs-marina experiment done on day one.

Fri
Aug 8

Adventure day — 76 Falls

Cruise down to Seventy-Six Falls, a houseboat-accessible waterfall — swim, explore, cool off under it. Don't jump the falls themselves — the rocks are slick and people have died there. For the cliff-jumping crowd, the bluffs around our anchor cove do the job (nothing crazy). Anchor a scenic cove nearby for the night with a sunset cookout.

Sat
Aug 9

Float day + the marina test

Big lazy swim-and-float day off the back of the boat. Drift back north toward the dock and, if we're curious, swing through Harmon Creek — the lake's big raft-up party cove — and/or grab a boat-in meal at a marina. Now we've seen both sides.

Last night we anchor at Beaver Creek: quiet, cliff-lined, and it's the fuel + pump-out marina — so we top off here and it's an easy morning cruise back Sunday.

Sun
Aug 10

Cruise home

Short morning cruise from Beaver Creek back to State Dock, return the boat by 11am, then drive to BNA and fly home.

🧭 The cove-vs-marina test, built in

We spend night 1 totally on our own in a quiet cove, then Saturday we sample the busier marina/party-cove scene before committing our last night. By Saturday evening we'll know exactly which one we are — with zero risk, since we're anchored on our own terms the whole time.
